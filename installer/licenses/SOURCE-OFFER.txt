Corresponding source availability and distributor offer
=====================================================

Source archives collected for this build are provided in the installed
sources directory. SOURCES.md identifies their exact revisions, and
manifest.json records the staged files and hashes. The same notices and
local modifications may be copied and redistributed under their licenses.

For distributor-approved releases, the DroidBox distributor offers to any
third party a complete machine-readable copy of the corresponding source
for the GPL-covered software conveyed in that release, including required
build scripts and local modifications. This offer is valid for at least
three years after the distributor's last distribution of that release.
For GPLv3-covered software it also remains valid for as long as the
distributor offers spare parts or customer support for that product model.

Requests can be made through the distributor's project issue tracker:
https://github.com/HEARTLESS5413/EMULATOR-/issues

Include the installer version and SHA-256 so the distributor can identify
the corresponding source set. Electronic access is provided without charge.
If a physical source medium is requested, any charge will not exceed the
reasonable cost of physically performing source distribution, subject to
the applicable license. A request may be made by anyone entitled under that
license and does not require purchase of support or another product.

Release obligation
------------------
This is the release offer text, not a claim that a development build's
inventory has been reviewed. Before making a release, the distributor must
retain and be able to supply the complete corresponding sources for the
actual payload, verify this request channel is usable, and complete the
release inventory. BUILD.md describes the enforced distribution-build gate.
An unreviewed development installer must not be published as a completed
release on the strength of this offer text.
