Sources and offers
------------------
Read SOURCE-OFFER.txt and SOURCES.md, installed alongside this notice.
Collected, pinned source archives and Sabi's local image modifications
are installed in the sources directory. Additional upstream notices and Qt
SBOMs are in licenses/upstream. manifest.json lists payload hashes/versions
and its release-inventory status.

Development builds require the source/notices review described in BUILD.md
before public redistribution. Inclusion of this license screen alone does
not establish that an unreviewed development payload is ready to distribute.
